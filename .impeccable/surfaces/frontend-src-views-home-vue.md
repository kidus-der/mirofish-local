## Direction contract

THESIS: A private harbour for charting how a question moves through a small society. Refuse the generic AI dashboard and the hacker terminal.

OWN-WORLD: Chart-water blue, deep sounding navy, chart-land buff, warm chart paper, notice magenta and buoy red. Alegreya for headings, Alegreya Sans for interface text, Alegreya Sans SC for compact labels. Thin chart rules, square controls, tabular figures, no nautical icons or ornament.

STORY: The researcher starts with documents and a question, sees privacy and model status, follows the run, then checks what came from source material and what came from simulation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact top bar with product name, navigation, language and local status. New scenario form at the centre, five-stage workflow on the left, recent runs and a chart preview at the right. Buoy red is reserved for actions.
